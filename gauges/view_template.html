What was guessed

  1. The drive: wheels3's 37 mm 30:1 gearmotor, 1.0 N m stall at the wheel and 330 rpm, from a datasheet, scaled by the search: 0.4 to 2.5 times the gearing (torque up, speed down; G3 doesn't read the speed it costs). The drive search also scaled the motor 0.5 to 2 times (its torque, rotor and 205 g mass together) and kept it near 1; the lean search holds it there. Every wheel has one. Tracks under the Trainer's 131 mm put the motor into the hull, flagged "repack".
  2. The insides: chassis-v2's Trainer parts and masses (3.2 kg), with the two battery packs free to move ±80 mm along and up to 15 mm up.
  3. The skin: one closed 1.5 mm PLA shell that replaces the Trainer's walls (about 416 g out) and holds every part inside, the camera head too for now. Never printed at this size. Where it meets a tyre the sim gives it a wheel well.
  4. The tail: two servos (a turntable and one pitch joint) of 0.3 to 4 N m stall, MG996R to 40 kg cm class, at 6 rad/s; each 45 g plus 8 g per N m. The link weighs 0.3 g a mm, the rubber foot 15 g. Only the 9 g MG90S class is on hand.
  5. The tail's moves are scripted reflexes: after a fall, swing through the ground and push, trying the plane toward the ground, then each side; on a step, push down behind when it stalls. A learned policy should do better.
  6. The floor: hard, with the ball's measured bounce; friction 0.6 floor, 0.9 tyre and 0.4 body, scaled ×0.65 to ×1.5. No tyre grip has been measured yet.
  7. The screen's bar: 40 falls at ×1 reading at least 0.85, so that designs near 0.75 aren't let through on luck. The finals read 200 falls at every friction.
