What changed in set 2

was stuck

Small gear is its own part

It was moulded onto the drum, 22.6 mm across, under a 10.5 mm hole. Now it pushes up onto a D-shaped stub (one flat side) from below, with one M3 × 8 button head.

was closed

Floor comes off

The gear bay was a closed box. Its floor is now a plate on 4 × M2 × 6, so both gears go in from underneath.

Well and gear bay fused

Nowhere on the well could take a screw, so they print as one base.

would jam

Gear play

The teeth touched on both sides. The small gear is now 0.3 mm smaller all round: 1.9° of play at the head as drawn, about 1° printed.

would drag

Bearing sits on its outer ring only

The 6809's turning inner ring sat on the fixed ledge. A 0.4 mm recess now clears it. Seat 58.1, hub 44.9, and a sharp step where it seats on the drum.

Cradle to drum (unchanged)

fixed

Three bolt holes

M3 × 12 at 20 mm out, 0°/120°/240°, matching the drum. A 3.4 hole through the bottom 5.5 mm, then a 6.4 recess open to the bowl, so a driver reaches from above before the ball goes in.

fixed

Ribbon path open

The groove now drops straight into the drum's 18 mm hole. The ribbon's full 16 mm width is clear the whole way, and the tightest bend is 6.3 mm radius. The old horn pocket and pin hole are gone.

fixed

Groove on the centre line

It used to land 9 mm forward, at the hole's edge. One soft 45° fold at the drum's side slot then turns the ribbon on edge, so no twist and no fold pocket are needed. Bolt the drum with that slot facing forward.

fine

Nothing collides

The coil's clash check reads 0.00 mm³ for the cradle against all six coil parts. The cradle's base and the drum's disc are the same size (Ø51).

no longer needed

The 40 mm twist

The orange ghost is the run a quarter twist would have needed, running up into the ball. One fold at the drum replaces it.
