+Y hub as a sleeve

The cradle's +Y arm snaps because the hub on your ball_plus stands 5.8 mm past the ball's flat face, into the collar. The fix you picked: the hub comes off and is printed as a sleeve that goes in from outside after the ball, like the −Y stub. The print files are in head-v2/out/print-rev2/ (plate_plus_sleeve.3mf). Use the slider to put the head together, and switch to Today to watch the arm bend. All parts sit where your geometry puts them (mm, Z up from the deck).

Why the arm bends

The hub (r 8.0–9.95) runs from the flat face at Y 24.5 out to 30.3. The collar's inner face is at Y 25.1. The −Y side gives under 1 mm, so the +Y arm has to spread about 5 mm while the hub goes past, then snap back round it.

chosen

What changes

  • ball_plus (your rev 2, IR cutout extended): the hub cut off flush, and a 1.5 mm socket in the flat face, with a flat on the lens side as a key.
  • Sleeve (new, ~10 min print): your hub's bore (16.0) and the notch for the servo's gear bump, 8.6 long. The 6704 presses on (20.0 or 20.1, two to try) up to a lip, so the bearing really turns.
  • Unchanged: cradle, post v3, both 6704s, the servo, the −Y stub.

Putting it together

  1. Ball straight down into the nest. Nothing bends.
  2. On the bench, press the 6704 onto the sleeve from the flat end until it stops on the lip.
  3. Sleeve and bearing together in from outside, flat to the lens side, until the sleeve drops into the socket.
  4. Post v3 on, 3 × M2 as before.
  5. −Y stub in, 3 × M2 × 6 as before.
