MG90S horns (2026-10-10). From Zach's download "mg90s-dimension-reference-measured-1.snapshot.1.zip"
(a measured TowerPro MG90S model with its three horns). MG90_Arm.step holds the three horns as exact solids;
solid 1 (x 26.55..33.45) is the double-arm horn, the same as MG90s_Arm02.stl.

Zach's caliper on his own double-arm horn matched the model (2026-10-10): 31.4 tip to tip, arms 1.65 thick,
hub 6.9 across, 3.7 from the arm's flat face to the hub's top.

Measured on the model (head-v2 frame notes in pan_coil_b.HORN_*): from the flat face up, a screw counterbore
~4.6 across to 1.0, a 0.4 web to 1.4, then the spline socket to 3.7.

Used by head-v2/fixes/gear_horn_set9.py for the input gear's pocket (zero offset, his rule).

Not tracked (2026-10-26): the download's licence wasn't recorded (the .snapshot.1 name looks like a GrabCAD
download), so MG90_Arm.step and MG90s_Arm02.stl are kept locally and gitignored, not redistributed. A fresh clone
needs that download saved here under the same names.
