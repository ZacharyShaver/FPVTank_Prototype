Orange is the camera ribbon: down off the board, back under it, up to the tilt axis, out through the hollow hub into a loose two-turn coil in the left pod, then down the stem, across the turntable and down the pedestal. Tilting winds and unwinds the coil instead of twisting the ribbon.

Blocky concept, not CAD. Board, hole pitch and holder height are measured; the lens and its field of view aren't modelled yet.
