How it works

What goes where?
The Pi crosswise at the back, with its header facing the middle. A pack along each wall. The two ESCs down the middle, with the PCA on one and the ESP32 on the other. The IMU between them, and the small boards on the Pi's top and the packs' tops.
Why this way?
What holds it upside down?
The lid. Every stack reaches it, so it presses straight down through the boxes' walls to the floor, and the pegs only stop things sliding. Turn on Upside down to look. With the lid off, the ridges hold each part in, and that's untested.
How do you swap a pack?
Lid off. Pull that pack's top, which brings its spacer and the small box on it. Lift the pack past the ridges. The lead comes out the back end, into the lane behind the packs.
How do you get at the Pi?
Lid off, pull its two stops, and your box slides out toward +y. The wall is in the way as drawn, so it has to lift out with its sleeve. That's a problem to look at.
