The blocks: who goes where

  1. Small-peg block: the same 5 mm peg as the frame. Any row; use 5.1.
  2. Big-peg block: a 6 mm peg. Only the one big hole by the rows.
  3. Block with a hole through it, and the loose pin: set the block over a 5.1 hole, then drop the pin through both.

To test one: stand the patch's edge against a wall, with the block on it. Push the block toward the wall with the heel of your hand and build up. About 22 kg is the sim's worst case. Wear glasses, and tell me what gives first: the peg, the plate, or nothing.
