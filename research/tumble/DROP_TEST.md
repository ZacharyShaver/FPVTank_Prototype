# Drop test: what to do (about 15 minutes per model)

**You need:**
- the printed test bodies;
- their spacers;
- 12 US quarters per body;
- a strip of tape;
- a table with a clear, flat, hard top;
- a ruler or a mark 6 cm above the table;
- your phone, for notes.

**Before you start.** The simulation's predictions for every drop below are already committed in git. Don't look
at them until you're done.

## Load the body
1. Turn the body upside down. The pocket opens in the base, so whatever goes in first ends up at the top of the
   body.
2. **Mass low:** push in the spacer first, slot end last, so the slot faces you. Then drop in the 12 quarters; they
   sit by the base. Tape over the opening.

## Drop it, 30 times
Do 5 drops from each of these six starting attitudes:

| # | Starting attitude | How to hold it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | nose down | point the head end straight down |
| 2 | tail down | point the back end straight down |
| 3 | left side down | its left side facing the table |
| 4 | right side down | its right side facing the table |
| 5 | upside down | base facing the ceiling |
| 6 | upside down, 45° to the left | halfway between 5 and 3 |

Hold it so its lowest point is 6 cm above the table and let go. Don't throw it or spin it. Record where it ends up:
**upright**, **inverted**, **left side**, **right side**, **nose up** or **nose down**. Use whichever face it rests on.
One line per drop, like `3, left side`.

## Then move the mass and do it again
3. Take out the tape. Tip out the coins, then pull out the spacer by its slot.
4. **Mass high:** with the body upside down, drop in the 12 quarters first; they fall to the top of the body. Then push
   in the spacer, slot end last. Tape over.
5. Repeat the 30 drops.

Send me the lines. I'll score them against the predictions and commit both.
