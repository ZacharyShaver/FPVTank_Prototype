# Drop test: E4_weeble from the stand (about 30 minutes)

E4 is the shape the self-righting search picked (`shell3.py`, `search3.py`). Its predictions are committed in
`results/drop_predictions_15cm_E4_weeble_*.json`. Don't look at them until you're done.

**You need:** E4, its spacer, the 12 quarters, tape, the drop stand at 150 mm, and your phone for notes and
(if you can) video.

**Which end is the nose:** the small dimple on the side, halfway up, marks the **nose** (the head end). Call
the starts and the rest states by the dimple: "nose down" means the dimple end points at the floor. That fixes the
nose/tail swap of runs 1-4.

## Load it
1. Turn E4 upside down (base up). The pocket opens in the base.
2. **Mass low:** push in the spacer first, slot end last. Then drop in the 12 quarters; they sit by the base.
   Tape over the opening (two crossed strips, as before).

## The stand
Every start uses the same setting: **post hole 280, sleeve's lower hole**. Grip E4 at its widest across the jaws.
Set the lowest point at the gauge (150 mm), let it settle, pinch.

## Drop it, 30 times
Five drops from each start, in this order:

| # | start | how |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | nose down | dimple end at the floor |
| 2 | tail down | dimple end up |
| 3 | left side down | one side at the floor (E4 is mirror-symmetric, so either) |
| 4 | right side down | the other side at the floor |
| 5 | upside down | base at the ceiling |
| 6 | upside down, 45° to the left | halfway between 5 and 3 |

Mark each landing as before: **o** upright, **x** upside down, **l / r** on a side, **n** on its nose (dimple down),
**t** on its tail (dimple up). If it rocks for a while, mark where it ends up once it stops.

## Then the control: mass high
3. Untape, tip out the coins, pull out the spacer.
4. **Mass high:** base up, drop in the 12 quarters first (they fall to the top), then the spacer, slot end last.
   Tape over.
5. The same 30 drops.

Send me the two blocks of 30 marks, as before. If a drop goes wrong (tape split, coins out, a bad release), mark
it `?` and redo it.
