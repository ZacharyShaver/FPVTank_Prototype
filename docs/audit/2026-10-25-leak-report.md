**Calls already made:**
- Fusion archives are ignored (Zach, 2026-10-25: nothing in them leaks). They are allowlisted as `unreadable *.f3d`;
  Python 3.10 can't read their zstd members.
- `--staged` scans only what is staged, not the whole index: about a second per commit instead of 30, and committed
  findings aren't flagged again.
- Each report row lists every path it covers.

**Known gaps** (what the scanner doesn't see):
- Credential names other than ssid, password, pwd, psk and pass. A generic `api_key=`, `token=` or `secret=` is caught
  only when the value has a known token prefix. MicroPython's positional `wlan.connect(...)` isn't matched either.
- A path after the escapes `\u000a`, `\x0a`, `\f`, `\b`, `\v` or `\0`, and text glued to a base64 run.
- Annotated-tag messages, and refs that point straight at a blob or tree.
- PDF authors in hex, UTF-16 or XMP; XMP GPS tags; Orca 3MF `.config` members; the zstd members of `.f3d` files.
- Binary types with no reader (gif, webp, woff2, wasm, pkl, npz, glb): reported as 'unreadable' only by `--path`.
