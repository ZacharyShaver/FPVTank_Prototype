Scorpion: chassis concept

A brainstorm, not a print set. Your TEST-PRINT-2 body and the v1 legs are in exactly, bolted on by their real screw holes; head v2 and every part the robot carries are placed; the new parts (green-grey) are what it takes to fit them all. Click any part to name it. Orange numbers are guesses.

The idea

  • Your band and legs stay. The v1 pods, outer frames, sprockets, rollers and motors bolt on unmodified. Your body only loses material (5 named cuts).
  • It grows up, not out. A hull over the tracks (sponsons) and a power bay add 2.1 L to your body's 1.4 L, without touching the legs. The deck sits where the head's well tops out, so the head turns in a ring flush with the deck.
  • Three zones, front to back: the head; the brain (Pi + Hailo + PCA9685 on a mezzanine over the packs); the power bay (ESCs, UBEC, switches, alarm, e-stop).
  • The tail earns its place. It holds the WiFi antenna 100 mm over the deck in an RF-clear stinger, away from the packs and motors, and folds flat.
  • Batteries come out the back. A door under the tail; both packs slide out along a spine. No screws to charge or swap.

Sizes

On board now, not in TEST-PRINT-2

  • 2 drive motors + 4:1 bevels, inside the pods (as v1). They stand ~4 mm into the body through the pods' windows.
  • 2 ESCs, power bay, LEDs behind the back panel. 46 × 34 × 18
  • UBEC 5 V / 5 A for the Pi only, power bay. 45 × 26 × 12
  • INA219 pack monitor (jumper it off 0x40), LiPo alarm on the back panel, Pi switch.
  • E-stop on the rear deck: cuts both ESC feeds, leaves the Pi up.
  • M.2 HAT+ with the Hailo-8L over the Pi's cooler. stack 30 tall
  • PCA9685 (your Adafruit file) beside the Pi.
  • USB WiFi + external antenna: adapter in the power bay, antenna in the tail.
  • Accessory rail, 118 long on the spine, and 70 × 118 × 75 kept clear over it.
  • Headlight / IR pods in the glacis; charge and balance ports on the back.

How it goes together

  1. Cut your body (the 5 CUT_ bodies in the STEP).
  2. Motor and ESC lead onto each pod, then the pods onto the body: 3 × M3 × 12 top, 3 × M3 × 10 bottom, from inside (the v1 guide's high version).
  3. Outer frames, sprockets, rollers, tracks: v1 as built.
  4. Mezzanine on its posts and spine.
  5. Power bay on the back of the body; ESCs, UBEC, switches into it.
  6. Pi + HAT and the PCA9685 onto the mezzanine; head base bolts to its front.
  7. Turret section on (stays on), then the drum, cradle and ball.
  8. Shell on: magnets or 2 thumbscrews. Rail on the shell, tail on its clevis.
  9. Packs in from the back, door shut.

Fit checks

Body material overlapping, counted in 1 mm cubes. Zero is clear; each control must read above zero, or the check would be blind.

Guesses to confirm

  • ESC 46 × 34 × 18 and UBEC 45 × 26 × 12: caliper yours (or name the UBEC).
  • Motor can Ø36 × 52, sitting 14 mm into the pod from its closed face.
  • HAT+ standoffs 16 mm over the Pi; the stack's real height.
  • Batteries 141 × 45.7 × 26: your model's numbers. Measured?
  • Head axis X 100 (my pick from the placement page; you haven't set it).
  • Deck Z 86, set by the head's well top with the base standing on the mezzanine.
  • The rail: assumed a Picatinny-style accessory rail. What is it?
  • Sprocket width on the axle and the track's lugs: drawn, not measured.

Open questions

  • The rail: what mounts on it (arm? launcher? sensors?), how long, and does it need power or signal?
  • Camera ribbon: 30 cm from the well's side to the Pi's CSI port, after the coil takes its share. The Pi sits as far forward as the mezzanine allows; a 50 cm ribbon removes the worry.
  • The tracks run 1.8° nose-down to the body: the pod's open side is turned 1.83° in the v1 files. On the floor the body sits nose-up 1.8°. Intended by the v1 designer?
  • Departure angle is ~25° behind (the power bay hangs 37 mm past the rear sprocket). Fine for floors; for kerbs, shorten it or chamfer under the door.
  • The tail fills part of the camera's view looking aft. Stinger could carry a rear camera instead.
  • The arm: on the rail? It wants its own UBEC: the sponsons have room.
