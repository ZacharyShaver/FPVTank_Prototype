The drive

Why not the 540 and the 4:1 bevel: at 2S the 55T can spins at about 7,000 rpm, so the sprocket turns at about 1,700. That's more than 4 m/s, ten times what the robot needs. It has to crawl at a sliver of throttle, where it has little torque, and the printed pinion wore through once.

What climbing asks for: 2.9 kg up 40° plus track drag is about 10 N per side, or 2.6 kg·cm at the sprocket. Aim for 8–10 kg·cm at stall, and about 200 rpm for 0.5 m/s.

So: a 37 mm metal gearmotor, about 30:1, with an encoder. It turns about 200 rpm at 7.4 V with about 8–9 kg·cm at stall, and the encoder gives the robot odometry for mapping later. It lies across the tail, its gearbox through the hull wall and the leg's frame. Its shaft drives a hub tube that turns in a bearing in the frame, so the track's pull goes into the frame, not the gearbox. Your brushed ESCs drive it as they drive the 540.

    Shopping list

    Fit checks

    Overlap in 1 mm cubes. Zero is clear; each control must read above zero, or the check would be blind.

    Guesses to confirm

    • The gearmotor: Ø37 gearbox 22 long, motor Ø34.5 × 33, encoder 11, 6 mm D shaft 15.5 long, 205 g. From datasheets: buy one, caliper it, and only the motor hole and the hub change.
    • Its speed and torque at 7.4 V: 200 rpm, 8–9 kg·cm.
    • TPU spring pads give the wheels 12 up / 5 down; the spring rate needs a test print.
    • Track band 3 mm thick with 3 mm grousers and 6 mm horns: ~158 g each. A thinner band or a lattice could save 50 g a side.
    • Masses as on the rugged page (walls 90 % solid, packs 300 g).

    Questions for you

    • Which gearmotor? A Pololu 37D 30:1 with encoder is the known quantity; a JGB37-520 is a third of the price. Either way, buy one first.
    • Track width: 52 mm makes the robot 296 wide and the self-righting cage 228 tall. 44 mm would save about 16 and 8.
    • Rear drive puts the motors in the tail (the front holds the head). The flippers then pivot on the front idler and get their own drive shaft through the hollow axle. OK?
    • First test print: one road wheel arm with its spring pad, a 10-link strip of track, and the sprocket, on a bench frame?
