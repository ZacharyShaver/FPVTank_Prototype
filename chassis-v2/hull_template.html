Scorpion: hull redesign

A new body, designed round the v1 legs (bolted on unmodified, by their 6 screws) and round everything the robot carries. The head drops to the floor, the hull rises behind it, the tracks run level. A brainstorm: click any part to name it; orange numbers are guesses. It replaces the first concept, which only wrapped covers round your TEST-PRINT-2 body.

The idea

  • Head low, in the nose. The pan coil base stands on the floor, so the turret deck sits at the well's top, 30 mm lower than before. The lens and the centre of mass come down with it, and the claws guard the ball.
  • Hull rises behind the head, out of the cradle's sweep: a carapace whose sponsons carry the ESCs straight over their motors, a brain bay under a lift-off hatch, a power bay at the back.
  • Tracks run level. The v1 outer frame tilts the tracks 1.8°; the new hull carries the pods' hole pattern turned 1.82° to cancel it, so the pan axis stands vertical and the horizon stays flat.
  • Packs low and aft, on the floor either side of a spine; they slide out the back door. The brain tray lifts out in one piece above them.
  • The tail earns its place: the WiFi antenna rides in the stinger, 100 mm over the deck, away from packs and motors.

Sizes

The parts, and how they print

  • Tub: floor, walls, nose, pods' pads, spine, ledges. One print, 272 × 132 × 50, floor down.
  • Carapace: sponsons, brain bay, power bay, back panel, tail clevis. 186 × 243, deck down.
  • Turret deck and two front fenders: plates.
  • Brain hatch; brain tray; two claws; five tail segments and the stinger.
  • All fit the K2 Pro's 300 mm bed. Heat-set M3 inserts where parts screw together; magnets on the hatch.

How it goes together

  1. Motor and ESC leads onto each pod (v1), then the pods onto the tub's pads: the v1 screws, from inside.
  2. Outer frames, sprockets, rollers, tracks: as v1.
  3. Head base onto its 4 floor inserts; front fenders, claws, turret deck; drum, cradle, ball.
  4. Packs in along the spine; brain tray onto its ledges; Pi + HAT and PCA9685 on it.
  5. Carapace onto the tub's rim, ESCs and power parts already in it; connectors through the bay wall.
  6. Hatch on (magnets), tail onto its clevis. Door shut.

Fit checks

Overlap, counted in 1 mm cubes. Zero is clear; each control must read above zero, or the check would be blind.
